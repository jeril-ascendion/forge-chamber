FAQ

Do I need to install Python or Docker?
No. Everything is bundled in the installer. Just download and run.
Where is my data stored?
Locally on your machine in %APPDATA%/ForgeChamber/. Session transcripts, skill scores, and settings never leave your device.
How do I update?
The app checks for updates automatically on startup. When a new version is available, you'll see a banner with a one-click update button.
